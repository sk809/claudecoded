How it works

Three steps. One finished film.

  1. Step 1

    Install the styles

    In your terminal, add the style library to Claude Code:

    claude plugin marketplace add lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscarclaude plugin install lemo-opuscar@lemolab
  2. Step 2

    Pick a style

    Choose one of the 59 styles here, or change one into your own with your brand colours, characters and tone.

  3. Step 3

    Say what you want

    Name the style and the story. Claude directs, draws, scores and mixes it, and can stop once to show you a storyboard first.

Rendering runs on your machine with your own Claude account, so there are no per-video credits.

FAQ

Frequently asked

Is this AI video generation?

No. Claude writes code that draws every frame on a canvas or in WebGL, composes the music from free sample libraries and narrates with text-to-speech. There is no video model and no stock footage.

What do I need to get started?

A computer running Mac, Windows or Linux, and Claude Code with a Claude Pro or Max plan. Install the style library with the two commands in How it works.

Can I make a style my own?

Yes. Ask Claude to change any library style, for example your own version of Ukiyo-e with your brand colours and characters, or start from a blank page. Your style is saved and reused for later films.

Can I use my own script, voice or music?

Yes. Claude asks up front whether you have your own voice, music or other material, and builds the film around it.

Who owns the films I make?

You do. Films you make with the library are yours with no credit needed. The demo films shown here are by LemoLab under CC BY 4.0.

How does billing work?

Pay monthly or yearly. You can switch plans or cancel at any time from your account page.
